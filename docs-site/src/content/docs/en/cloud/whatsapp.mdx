With **Open Factura Cloud** you can invoice from WhatsApp, without opening the dashboard:

- Link your phone to your user from the dashboard.
- Issue invoices and tax credit invoices with guided menus: customer, products, and a summary
  validated against Hacienda's rules before you confirm.
- Receive the PDF in the same conversation and look up your latest documents.
- The bot acts with the same permissions as your user, in your company's active environment.
- Add allowed phones so other people can issue on behalf of your company without a dashboard user.
  Each phone gets a WhatsApp invitation with your company name and the last digits of your NIT, and
  it only issues once its owner taps «Aceptar» (Accept). The dashboard shows whether it is pending,
  active or declined, and you can resend the invitation (up to 3 per phone a day). If they decline,
  you cannot invite them again for 30 days.
- The name and NIT are what the company declared. The last digits of the NIT only appear if the
  company uploaded its Hacienda certificate (which is tied to that NIT); otherwise the invitation
  says «NIT: sin verificar» (not verified).
- An invitation never blocks the bot: if you already work with another company, you see it once and
  carry on with your menu.
- If the phone has not messaged the Open Factura number in the last 24 hours, WhatsApp may not
  deliver the invitation: ask them to send «hola» to the number and they will see it there.

This feature is exclusive to the managed version and is not part of the open-source code. See the
plans at [openfactura.co](https://openfactura.co).
