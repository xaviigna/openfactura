With **Open Factura Cloud** you can invoice from WhatsApp, without opening the dashboard:

- Link your phone to your user from the dashboard.
- Issue invoices and tax credit invoices with guided menus: customer, products, and a summary
  validated against Hacienda's rules before you confirm.
- Receive the PDF in the same conversation and look up your latest documents.
- The bot acts with the same permissions as your user, in your company's active environment.

This feature is exclusive to the managed version and is not part of the open-source code. See the
plans at [openfactura.co](https://openfactura.co).
